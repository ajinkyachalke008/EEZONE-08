# ⚡ EE ZONE — Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

EE ZONE was built on the belief that engineering knowledge should be accessible to everyone, everywhere. In that spirit, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a **harassment-free, respectful, and inclusive experience** for everyone — regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, religion, or any other personal characteristic.

---

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and technical approaches
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the engineering community
- Showing empathy and support toward other contributors
- Crediting others' work and ideas appropriately

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Dismissing beginners or students for asking questions
- Plagiarizing code or contributions without attribution
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and any community spaces where EE ZONE is represented.
